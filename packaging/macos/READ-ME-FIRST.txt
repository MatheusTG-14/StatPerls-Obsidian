StatPearls Vault for Mac
========================

FIRST TIME ONLY: macOS blocks programs from the internet that are not signed by Apple.
This one is not signed. To allow it:

  1. Open the Terminal app (press Cmd+Space, type "Terminal", press Return).
  2. Type  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine   then a space, then drag this whole
     "StatPearls Vault" folder onto the Terminal window, and press Return.

Then double-click "Start StatPearls Vault.command" and choose Install.
(Alternative: Control-click the file, choose Open, then Open again.)

Pick a folder that is NOT in iCloud Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, for example a new
folder in your home folder. You need about 10 GB free and Obsidian closed.
The first install takes about an hour.

This program contains no StatPearls text: it downloads the articles from the
U.S. National Library of Medicine onto your Mac. Do not share the vault.
Educational use only, not for medical decisions about patients.
